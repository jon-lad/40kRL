# Reference Material

This project implements a roguelike based on the **Rogue Trader RPG** by Fantasy Flight Games. When interpreting user requirements, feature requests, or spec descriptions, consult the reference documents in `Reference/` to understand game mechanics, creature stats, weapon profiles, and setting context.

## How to use

When the user mentions RPG mechanics (e.g., "weapon training", "hit locations", "degrees of success", "critical effects", "penetration", "tearing", "unbalanced"), look up the relevant rules in the reference documents to produce accurate requirements.

## Key reference documents

- `Reference/340049081-Rogue-Trader-The-Koronus-Bestiary.txt` — Creature stat blocks, xenos races (Eldar, Orks, Rak'Gol, Stryxis, Yu'Vath), weapon tables, traits, and the Xenos Generator system.

## Common lookups

- **Ork weapons table** — Big Shoota, Deffgun, Rokkit Launcha, Scorcha, Shokk Attack Gun, Snazzgun, Big Choppa, Grot Prod, Power Klaw, Tankhammer
- **Eldar weapons** — Shuriken Catapult/Pistol, Lasblaster, Fusion Gun, Reaper Launcher, Death Spinner, Prism Rifle, Banshee Power Sword, Wraithcannon
- **Creature traits** — Bestial, Brutal Charge, Fear, Natural Armour, Improved Natural Weapons, Unnatural Strength/Toughness, Quadruped, Flyer, Burrower, Regeneration, Stuff of Nightmares, Daemonic, Warp Instability
- **Ork traits** — Make It Work (Unreliable weapons aren't Unreliable in Ork hands), Mob Rule (+10 WP per nearby Ork), Might Makes Right (Intimidate for Command)
- **Species Traits (Xenos Generator)** — Apex, Armoured, Deadly, Mighty, Resilient, Swift, Venomous, Paralytic, Stealthy, Disturbing, Fade-Kind, Silicate, Warped, Unkillable, etc.

## Weapon qualities referenced

- **Tearing** — roll extra damage die, take highest
- **Razor Sharp** — double Penetration on 2+ DoS
- **Power Field** — field that destroys parrying weapons on certain rolls
- **Unbalanced** — –10 to Parry
- **Toxic** — extra Toughness test or take additional damage
- **Blast (X)** — area effect radius
- **Flame** — sets targets on fire
- **Inaccurate** — no bonus for aiming
- **Unreliable** — jams on 91+
- **Storm** — extra hits on full auto
