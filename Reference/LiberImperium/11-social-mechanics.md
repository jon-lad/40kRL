                              11.0 Social Mechanics
Fierce warriors though the player characters may be, conversation and non-violent interaction is an important aspect of life. Asking questions is a
big part of most adventures, and a group that lets its guns do all the talking is likely to end up with many corpses but few answers.
          Inevitably, the player characters find themselves interacting with varied and often strange inhabitants of the Imperium, whether by choice
or necessity. For the GM, the responsibility of portraying the numerous NPCs with whom the players interact may seem daunting. To assist the
GM in determining how the universe’s characters respond to the player’s characters, each NPC has a Disposition (and may have a Personality if he
wishes). Disposition (and the optional Personality ruleset) serve as useful guidelines for the GM when portraying an NPC, but also provide a way to
determine his reaction to the queries, bribes, and threats of the player characters. Disposition represents an NPC’s opinion of the player characters,
while Personality affects his behaviour during conversation and defines the effects of Social Skills used by them.
          Knowing how best to deal with these individuals can make all the difference between success and failure. It is important to keep in mind
that anyone could be a potential ally, contact, or co-conspirator, and treating them well can present a valuable resource to the characters’ efforts.

                                                             Interaction and Groups
When a player uses a Social Skill against a group, such as when commanding a ship’s crew or an allied warband, he resolves the test as described
earlier with the disposition of the group set by the GM based on the group’s overall attitude toward the players. The player makes the Test as normal,
and with one Degree of Success he affects a number of individuals in the group equal to his Fellowship Bonus. Note that the Air of Authority Talent
can increase the number of individual targets a player can affect.
