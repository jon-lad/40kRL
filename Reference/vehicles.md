## 13.3.9 Daemon Engines

> **Daemon Engine (X) Trait:** Gains From Beyond and The Stuff of Nightmares (immune to Fear, Pinning, Stunning, Insanity, Poison, Disease, and mind-affecting Psychic Powers). Increases Toughness Bonus by X (Force/Sanctified attacks ignore this). Protected by a Force Field (Protection Rating 30) that never Overloads but is bypassed by Sanctified/Force Qualities and by direct-damage Psychic Powers. Each Round recovers 1d5 Wounds or removes 1 Critical Damage. Weapons have unlimited Clip and never reload (but can Jam). Once per encounter may re-roll a full damage roll (each 9/10 on re-rolled dice inflicts 1d5 True Damage on the engine).
