# Rogue Trader Critical Effects Reference
## Optimized for AI consumption — critical injury tables

---

## How Critical Damage Works

1. When total damage accumulated ≥ character's Wounds, excess = Critical Damage
2. Look up Critical Damage amount on the table matching: **Damage Type** + **Hit Location**
3. Apply the effect listed
4. If character takes MORE damage while already in Critical, add to existing Critical total and apply new effect
5. Effects are cumulative unless mutually exclusive (use most recent)

### Death Threshold
- Most tables: death occurs at Critical Damage 7-10+ depending on location
- Head criticals are deadliest (death at lower values)
- Body criticals are next most dangerous
- Limb criticals tend toward maiming before death

## Conditions

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Blood Loss | Lose 1 Wound per round at end of turn until treated (First Aid or Medicae) |
| Stunned | Lose next turn; cannot take Reactions; -20 to Evasion |
| On Fire | Take 1d10 E damage (ignoring armour) per round; Ag test to extinguish |
| Prone | -10 to WS; -10 to be hit at range; Half Action to stand |
| Blinded | -30 to WS/BS; opponents gain +30 to hit |
| Deafened | -20 to Awareness (hearing); cannot respond to verbal commands |
| Fatigue (per level) | -10 to all tests; unconscious if levels ≥ TB |
