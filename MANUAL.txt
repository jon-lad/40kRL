40kRL MANUAL
Version 0.14.0

Movement
--------
  Arrow keys  Move in 4 directions (N/S/E/W)
  KP_1  Move south-west
  KP_2  Move south
  KP_3  Move south-east
  KP_4  Move west
  KP_5  End turn (wait in place)
  KP_6  Move east
  KP_7  Move north-west
  KP_8  Move north
  KP_9  Move north-east
  h  Move west (vi-key)
  j  Move south (vi-key)
  k  Move north (vi-key)
  l  Move east (vi-key)
  y  Move north-west (vi-key)
  u  Move north-east (vi-key)
  b  Move south-west (vi-key)
  n  Move south-east (vi-key)

Actions
-------
  g  Pick up item
  s  Shoot (ranged attack)
  r  Reload weapon
  o  Open door
  a  Aim (called shot)
  A  All-out attack
  R  Run (double move)
  C  Charge (move + melee)
  KP_5  End turn

Overlays
--------
  i  Inventory
  c  Character / equipment sheet
  l  Look mode
  x  Advances (XP spending)
  m  World map
  e  Equipment
  ?  Help (this screen)

Navigation
----------
  <  Ascend stairs
  >  Descend stairs
  ESC  Save and return to menu
