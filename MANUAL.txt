Movement
--------
  Arrow keys  Move in 4 directions (N/S/E/W)
  KP_1  Move south-west
  KP_2  Move south
  KP_3  Move south-east
  KP_4  Move west
  KP_5  End turn (wait in place)
  KP_6  Move east
  KP_7  Move north-west
  KP_8  Move north
  KP_9  Move north-east
  h  Move west (vi-key)
  j  Move south (vi-key)
  k  Move north (vi-key)
  l  Move east (vi-key)
  y  Move north-west (vi-key)
  u  Move north-east (vi-key)
  b  Move south-west (vi-key)
  n  Move south-east (vi-key)

Actions
-------
  g  Pick up item
  s  Shoot (ranged attack)
  r  Reload weapon
  o  Open door
  a  Aim (called shot)
  A  All-out attack
  R  Run (double move)
  C  Charge (move + melee)
  KP_5  End turn

Overlays
--------
  i  Inventory
  c  Character / equipment sheet
  l  Look mode
  x  Advances (XP spending)
  m  World map
  e  Equipment
  ?  Help (this screen)

Navigation
----------
  <  Ascend stairs
  >  Descend stairs
  ESC  Save and return to menu

Mechanics
---------
  NPC stat blocks
    Enemies carry their own skills, talents, and traits (defined in
    Enemies.lua) and are treated like the player in combat:
      - Dodge and Parry skills contribute defensive reactions against
        your attacks.
      - Weapon Training talents remove the unproficient penalty on
        their attacks, for both melee and ranged.
      - Traits drive real mechanics: Brutal Charge adds bonus charge
        damage, Sturdy grants knockdown immunity, and Size adjusts the
        to-hit roll against the target.
    Enemies never gain XP — they use fixed profiles in the style of
    the Rogue Trader bestiary.

  Trait-driven monster AI
    Some enemies react to the tide of battle:
      - Cowardly enemies flee when badly wounded.
      - Mob Rule enemies hold their ground when enough allies are
        nearby.

  High-score leaderboard
    Every run is recorded when your character dies: character name,
    career, rank, homeworld, total XP, deepest level reached, cause of
    death, and date. Runs are ranked by total XP, with the deepest
    level used as a tiebreak.
    View the leaderboard from the main menu's "High Scores" entry. When
    you die, press Enter to jump to your placement on the leaderboard —
    your entry is highlighted with its rank number — or the top 10 if
    your run did not place. High scores are stored separately from your
    save in highscores.dat, so starting a new game never clears them.
      - Enter        (death screen) Show your placement / the top 10
      - PgUp / PgDn  Scroll through the ranked runs
      - ESC / Enter  Return from the High Scores screen
